Functional & technical blueprint

Learner Attendance & Parent Monitoring System

Daily attendance for schools, home-to-school-to-home confirmation for guardians, and a strict rule about who may see what.

1. Purpose

The system exists to do nine things well:

  1. Record daily learner attendance accurately and efficiently.
  2. Let teachers mark learners Present, Absent, Late or Excused.
  3. Let guardians confirm when a child leaves home and when they return.
  4. Give teachers and guardians a structured channel for comments on behaviour.
  5. Give teachers access only to learner information their responsibilities require.
  6. Ensure guardians access only information belonging to their own children.
  7. Generate attendance, lateness and behaviour reports for school management.
  8. Provide timely notifications to guardians and staff where necessary.
  9. Protect learner and family information through strong privacy and security controls.

Points 3 and 6 together are the point: the whole journey, visible only to those entitled to see it.

2. Roles and access

Three roles, with a deliberately narrow definition of what each may reach.

Administrator

Head teacher, deputy, school office. Registration, every record, reports, settings and the audit log.

Teacher

Only the learners in their assigned classes. The assignment also carries a role, and that distinction matters: a class teacher takes the register; a subject teacher on the same class may view learners and record behaviour, but not attendance. Remove the assignment and the access ends at once.

Parent / Guardian

Only learners linked to them by an authorised, school-verified link. They confirm departure and arrival, see attendance and lateness, read and reply to shared behaviour notes, and update their own contact details. Nothing else — no other learner, no other guardian.

3. Modules

3.1 School administration

  • Create and manage the school profile.
  • Register classes, streams and academic terms.
  • Register learners and assign admission numbers.
  • Register teachers and assign them to classes and subjects.
  • Register guardians and link them to their children.
  • Manage user roles and permissions.
  • View school-wide attendance statistics.
  • Generate and export attendance and behaviour reports.
  • Maintain an audit log of important actions and changes.

3.2 Teacher module

  • Secure sign-in.
  • View assigned classes and learners.
  • Take the daily register; mark Present, Absent, Late, Excused or School activity.
  • Record arrival time where the school requires it.
  • Record behaviour: positive observations, concerns and incidents.
  • View attendance history for authorised learners.
  • Respond to relevant guardian comments.
  • Receive alerts and school announcements.

3.3 Parent / guardian module

  • Secure sign-in.
  • View only the linked child or children.
  • Confirm and comment when a child leaves home for school.
  • Confirm and comment when the child returns home.
  • View daily and historical attendance, lateness and absence.
  • View authorised teacher behaviour comments and respond to them.
  • Receive notifications about attendance, lateness, behaviour and school messages.
  • Update approved contact details.

4. The daily home → school → home workflow

Guardian

Child leaves home

Records “left home for school” with the time and an optional comment.

Teacher

Child arrives at school

The class teacher records attendance for the whole class.

System

Attendance alert

If a learner is not marked present by the cut-off, the guardian is notified.

School

School departure

Optionally, release from school is recorded at the end of the day.

Guardian

Child arrives home

Confirms the child is home, with the time and an optional comment.

System

Daily record

The complete authorised record is stored for reporting and follow-up.

The gap this closes. A confirmed departure with no arrival on the register means a child who left home and did not reach school. The register screen lists exactly those learners before it can be submitted, and the cut-off alert raises it even if nobody looks.

5. Learner profile

Each learner record holds:

  • Admission number and full name
  • Class and stream
  • Date of birth
  • A photograph only where lawfully collected and necessary
  • Parent/guardian linkage and relationship
  • Emergency contact information
  • Attendance history and late-coming history
  • Behaviour records, teacher comments and guardian comments
  • Other school information the school approves

7. Notifications

  • Learner marked absent
  • Learner marked late
  • Attendance not recorded by the defined cut-off time
  • A teacher records a behaviour concern
  • A teacher responds to a guardian comment
  • School announcements
  • Emergency notifications
  • Optional confirmation when a learner is released from school

Each notification is addressed to one recipient. Guardian notifications go only to an authorised guardian.

9. Security and privacy

The governing principle: everyone sees only what their role needs, enforced at the database and application levels — not in the interface. Hiding a link is not access control.

How that is implemented in practice:

  • Role-based access control, with authorisation resolved from the linkage and assignment tables on every query.
  • Row-level security in the database, so a query that omits the scope returns nothing rather than everything.
  • Server-side authorisation on every endpoint. The client is never trusted to filter; a request for another family’s learner is rejected by the API, not hidden by the app.
  • Least privilege for staff and administrators — including the class-teacher-only restriction on taking a register.
  • Secure authentication with a strong password policy, hashed credentials, expiring sessions and optional two-factor authentication.
  • Encryption in transit, with sensitive data protected at rest.
  • An audit trail of record creation, editing and deletion, visible to administrators and append-only.
  • Secure backups and a tested recovery procedure.
  • Privacy notices and appropriate consent and authorisation processes.
  • Data minimisation — no personal information beyond what the school’s stated purposes require. Learner photographs are optional, not assumed.
  • Compliance with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.
ⓘ Try it: sign in as a guardian, then edit the learner id in the address bar. The record is refused — the refusal does not depend on the screen.

10. Recommended technology

  • Web application — responsive, usable on a computer or a smartphone.
  • Mobile application — Android for teachers and guardians first; iOS later if required.
  • Backend — a secure REST or GraphQL API, or equivalent application server.
  • Database — PostgreSQL or MySQL.
  • Authentication — session or token based, with optional two-factor authentication.
  • Notifications — push, SMS and/or email, chosen per school by budget and need.
  • Hosting — reputable cloud infrastructure with automated backups and monitoring.

The prototype is dependency-free — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a browser-local store standing in for the API. It shows the behaviour, not the production architecture.

11. Delivery scope

The first version carries this much and no more:

  • School registration and administration
  • Teacher accounts
  • Parent/guardian accounts
  • Learner registration
  • Class and stream management
  • Daily attendance
  • Parent home-departure confirmation
  • Parent home-arrival confirmation
  • Behaviour comments
  • Notifications
  • Attendance reports
  • Strict role-based access

  • School fees and payment tracking
  • School transport and bus tracking

  • Homework and assignments
  • Examination management

  • AI-assisted attendance and trend reports, subject to privacy and school policy
